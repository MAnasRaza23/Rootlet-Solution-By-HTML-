Full-Stack Web Development Services

Full-stack engineering is a widely sought-after service in web development due to its comprehensive nature. Unlike super-specialized developers, full-stack engineers possess the expertise to develop both the front and back end of web applications using the most effective programming languages and technologies for each specific case.

Full-stack developers are highly skilled and experienced engineers. The full-stack approach is commonly employed for projects that necessitate end-to-end or full-cycle management, regardless of their size or complexity. Some of the most popular “stacks” (a.k.a. technologies) are:

  • MEAN Stack: MongoDB, Express, AngularJS, and Node.js.
  • MERN Stack: MongoDB, Express, ReactJS, and Node.js
  • Django Stack: Django, Python, and MySQL as Database.
  • Rails or Ruby on Rails: Ruby, PHP, and MySQL.
  • LAMP Stack: Linux, Apache, MySQL, and PHP.
